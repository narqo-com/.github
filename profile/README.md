# Narqo

**Free image tools that run in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.**

[narqo.com](https://narqo.com) converts, edits and checks images entirely on your own device: no upload, no account, no file limits, and it keeps working offline once a page has loaded.

### Convert images

- **HEIC (iPhone photos):** [HEIC to JPG](https://narqo.com/convert/heic-to-jpg) · [HEIC to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/heic-to-png) · [HEIF to JPG](https://narqo.com/convert/heif-to-jpg) · [HEIF to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/heif-to-png)
- **WebP and AVIF:** [WebP to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/webp-to-png) · [WebP to JPG](https://narqo.com/convert/webp-to-jpg) · [AVIF to JPG](https://narqo.com/convert/avif-to-jpg) · [AVIF to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/avif-to-png)
- **PNG and JPG:** [PNG to JPG](https://narqo.com/convert/png-to-jpg) · [PNG to WebP](https://narqo.com/convert/png-to-webp) · [JPG to WebP](https://narqo.com/convert/jpg-to-webp) · [JFIF to JPG](https://narqo.com/convert/jfif-to-jpg)
- **Other formats:** [SVG to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/svg-to-png) · [GIF to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/gif-to-png) · [BMP to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/bmp-to-png) · [ICO to PNG](https://narqo.com/convert/ico-to-png)
- [Image formats explained](https://narqo.com/formats): JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF and more, side by side

### Edit photos

- [Crop image](https://narqo.com/crop-image): aspect ratio presets, circle crop, straighten
- [Resize image](https://narqo.com/resize-image): exact sizes for social media, or by pixels
- [Compress image](https://narqo.com/compress-image): down to a target file size
- [Rotate or flip image](https://narqo.com/rotate-image)
- [Extract a frame from a video or Live Photo](https://narqo.com/extract-frame-from-video)
- [Photo editor](https://narqo.com/photo-editor) · [Photo collage maker](https://narqo.com/photo-collage-maker)

### Privacy and trust

- [Photo metadata (EXIF) viewer and remover](https://narqo.com/tools/photo-metadata): see and remove GPS location before you share a photo
- [AI image checker](https://narqo.com/ai-image-checker): reads C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC AI labels and generator settings inside an image
- [Watermark checker](https://narqo.com/watermark-checker) · [Invisible watermark](https://narqo.com/invisible-watermark) (Adobe TrustMark, in your browser)
